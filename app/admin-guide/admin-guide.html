Admin guide

For Lou, who runs the server: how the system fits together, how to manage it from the Mac through Claude, backups, updates, users, alerts and what to do when something goes wrong.

The short version

  • The PC pc-lou is the server. It runs everything by itself: backups, checks, automations, updates.
  • You manage it from the Mac: ask Claude in the conversation linked to the PC. The PC applies what Claude puts in place within 2 minutes.
  • Something is wrong? Help (?) → Status in the app says what, and what to do. First fix: restart the server.
  • Your data is in one file on the PC, backed up every night and copied to the Mac every day.

2Managing everything from the Mac

Open the Claude conversation linked to the PC (from the Mac or the phone) and ask in plain words. Claude puts files on the PC; the PC's task Tasks auto-update applies them within 2 minutes; Claude reads the result back to you.

Ask ClaudeWhat happens on the PC
"Change the app so that…"Claude changes the code, tests it, puts app-next.zip on the PC. It is installed (deploy-app, previous version kept, put back by itself if the new one does not answer). Your devices update themselves.
"Run a health check"health-check-now
"Restart the server"restart-server: Vikunja, app server, public addresses, then a check
"Make a backup"backup-now
"Update Vikunja" (optionally "to v2.8.0")update-vikunja: tested on a copy of your data first, goes back by itself if anything fails
"Repair the services"repair-services: re-creates scheduled tasks, Funnel, power settings
"Put the previous app back"rollback-app
"Mark these tasks done / move them"task-ops: validates, moves to a project and/or completes your own tasks (never deletes), with your account's key on the PC. Claude reads your tasks from a fresh backup first.
"Create a user: name, e-mail"create-user: the PC makes a random temporary password and sends it only to your Mac (Downloads, YYMMDD_Tasks-account-<username>.txt). Claude never sees it.

Only these eight requests (and a new app) are accepted; anything else is refused. Results: requests\<name>.result.txt, state\auto-update-last.txt, state\auto-update-history.log, and the Auto-update line in ? → Status.

Still needs someone at the PC

  • rollback-vikunja and restore-backup (they ask for confirmation on purpose).
  • Signing in to Windows after a full shutdown or a power cut; signing in to Tailscale again.
  • Anything needing a password: Claude never types passwords.

For it to work: the PC is on and online, the Claude desktop app is open on it (it starts with Windows), and the conversation is linked to the PC. If Claude says the PC cannot be reached, it is asleep, off, offline or the Claude app is closed.

3Leaving the PC on its own

Done once. Then the PC can stay locked in a corner.

  1. scripts\install-auto-update.bat as administrator (done).
  2. Charger plugged in. Lid closed is fine on mains power (repair-services sets "do nothing" on lid close).
  3. Windows: Settings → Accounts → Sign-in options → "Use my sign-in info to automatically finish setting up after an update": On. Settings → Windows Update → Advanced options → Active hours: your usual hours.
  4. Claude app open, signed in, starting with Windows (Settings → Apps → Startup → Claude: On).
  5. Leave it locked (Windows+L): not signed out, not shut down.
  6. Tailscale admin console (from any device): Disable key expiry for pc-lou.
  7. BIOS/UEFI: "power on after a power cut", if the PC has it.

When the PC is off anyway

Nothing is lost: every device keeps working offline and sends its changes when the PC is back. Other devices just don't see each other's changes until then. Automations and alerts from the PC pause.

5Health checks and alerts

Two levels only. Problem (red): act today. Warning (orange): when convenient. Each one says what to do, next to it in ? → Status.

  • Bar under the top bar: the worst one, "+N" if more. Phone (ntfy): generic texts only.
  • You see everyone's warnings: the server's, and those of every account (its devices, its automations), before they even tell you. Each one says Seen by: who has it on their screen (everyone; you and Alex; only you; only this device; nobody any more once hidden for everyone), Concerns: everyone (the server), you, or the account's name, and Cause: which device of whom (for example "Alex · phone (Firefox, …)"). The other users see only the server's warnings and their own.
  • "Got it" on a warning: Got it (just me) hides it for you only, on all your devices (the others still see it); Got it (everyone) hides it for everyone it concerns. On a warning that concerns only you there is a single "Got it". Another user's "Got it" only ever hides it for them. Either way it comes back if the situation changes. Problems (red) cannot be hidden. Kept in state\acknowledged.json (everyone) and state\acknowledged-users.json (per person). Your own "Got it" also silences your phone alerts and PC notifications for that warning.
  • Power: the PC really on battery for 1 h 30 (since 11 Nov 2026; one hour on 10 Nov), or at 20 % or less, is a problem (red), and your phone gets an urgent notification at once (ntfy priority 5), once per stage, checked every minute. Back on the charger: "all OK again". "Really": Windows says the charger is unplugged (its power line flag), and the charge has gone down by 3 points or more. On the night of 10–11 Nov the PC was plugged in and full, but the battery said "discharging" (it rests when full) and five false alarms were logged. Before 10 Nov it was a warning after 4 hours, checked hourly (9–10 Nov: unplugged around 20:00, switched off at 05:07). Each alert is logged in state\auto-update-history.log, with "sent" or "NOT sent (ntfy unreachable)".
  • What runs on the PC (since 12 Nov 2026, hourly, seen by you only): PC: the server's programs: a script of the server stuck far too long (the minute update over 45 min, the health check over 30 min, the others over 3 h), or a listener running twice, is stopped by itself (only the server's own; listed in state\pc-load-stopped.log). PC: busy programs: a program busy for 3 hours in a row (15 % of the processor or 1.5 GB of memory) is a warning. PC: start-up programs: those that start with Windows and are not needed (disable them in Task Manager › Startup apps, or "Got it"). Windows, its updates, the antivirus, the search index, the drivers, Tailscale, OneDrive and Claude are normal: never stopped, never reported as useless.
  • Error report (30 days) in ? → Status: everything over a month, to give Claude. Full list with thresholds: docs\ALERTS.md.

Getting the alerts on your phone, outside the app

The app itself cannot ring your phone when it is closed: the alerts go through the free ntfy app, subscribed to the PC's topic (in state\ntfy-topic.txt; keep it private).

  1. Install ntfy (Play Store, F-Droid or App Store).
  2. + → Subscribe to topic → type the topic exactly, server ntfy.sh (the default) → Subscribe.
  3. Android: allow its notifications; Settings → Apps → ntfy → Battery → Unrestricted (otherwise Android puts it to sleep and nothing arrives); in ntfy's own settings, keep "Instant delivery" on. iPhone: allow notifications, including Time Sensitive.
  4. Test: ? → Settings → Alerts → Test sends to your own alert topic; for the PC's topic, the next health warning, or ask Claude for a test.

On the Mac, move the copies out of Downloads from time to time and keep the last few: they contain everyone's tasks and the server secret.

Restoring

  1. At the PC: scripts\restore-backup.bat, choose the date. The current database is kept first, so it can be undone.
  2. Everything restarts. Devices then sync; changes they had not sent yet are sent on top.

Restoring affects every account on the server. For one lost task or project, look first in the Bin (30 days), or ask Claude to recover it from a backup without restoring everything.

8Other users

Each person gets a private account on your server, with the same app. They never see your tasks; you never see theirs in the app.

Adding someone

  1. From the Mac: ask Claude "create a user: <name>, <e-mail>". The PC creates the account with a random temporary password and sends it to your Mac's Downloads as YYMMDD_Tasks-account-<username>.txt, with what to tell them (Claude never sees the password). Or at the PC: scripts\create-user.bat. Sign-ups stay closed to everyone else.
  2. Pass on the temporary password by a private channel, then delete the text file. Give them the User guide (docs\Tasks-user-guide.pdf), the address, their username and temporary password.
  3. They install the app: Help (?) → Install the app installs it or shows the steps for their browser (guide, section 1). Then they change their password in Vikunja and should turn on two-factor.

What they get automatically

  • An Inbox project; the GTD automations on by default (the app creates a key limited to what they need; they can turn it off in ? → Settings → Automations, and that choice is kept).
  • Their own health line per account; "Download my data" with a monthly reminder.
  • Labels the automations need are created in their account if missing. p1, waiting-for, Pro/Perso, "Single tasks" projects: they create them if they want.

Be honest with them

  • Your PC is their server: when it is off, they work offline.
  • You can technically read their data (database, backups, Mac copies). Keep it to people you know.
  • Sign-in attempts are limited for everyone together (10 a minute): fine for a handful of people.

Removing someone

They can delete their own account in Vikunja (Settings). Otherwise ask Claude for the command for your server. Their data, automation key and automation memory go with it.

Other ways to share

Their own server: make-starter-kit gives a zip with none of your data; they run setup-new-pc on their own PC. A shared project: in Vikunja's pages, the project → Share (read-only or read-write). Details: docs\SHARING.md.

11Moving to a new PC

Give the new PC the same Tailscale name, pc-lou: then no device needs anything.

  1. Old PC working: export-for-new-pc. Old PC dead: take the newest tasks-server-<date>.zip from the Mac (and the newest tasks-data-<date>.db if it is more recent).
  2. Unpack into Documents on the new PC. If the .db is newer, put it in todo-server\vikunja\db\ as vikunja.db.
  3. scripts\setup-new-pc.bat (installs Python and Tailscale). In Tailscale: sign in, remove the old pc-lou, rename the new machine pc-lou.
  4. It runs repair-services, starts everything and checks. Then install-automations + start-automations (never on two PCs at once), install-weekly-safeguard, install-auto-update, install-alerts.
  5. Section 3 checklist; Claude app installed and linked.

Details, and what to change if the name cannot be pc-lou: docs\MOVE-TO-NEW-PC.md.

13Security

  • Public: only the Vikunja sign-in/API and the app's files, through Tailscale Funnel, in HTTPS. Nothing else on the PC or the home network.
  • In place: sign-ups closed, sign-in attempts limited, API only usable from the app (CORS), link sharing off, security headers, two-factor supported, Vikunja updates verified and tested, hourly security checks.
  • Your part: two-factor on your account; a long unique password; Windows password and updates; disk encryption (BitLocker) if available; delete unused API tokens.
  • Keep private: vikunja\run-vikunja.cmd (server secret), every *token*.txt in state\, backups\, the copies on the Mac, export zips, and the ntfy topic (anyone with it can read or fake alerts; texts are generic).
  • Anyone using the PC can read the database file: it holds every account.
  • Sessions (19 Nov 2026): a device stays signed in for a year after its last use (VIKUNJA_SERVICE_JWTTTLLONG=31536000 in run-vikunja.cmd; 30 days before). The access token itself still lasts 10 minutes and is renewed with a single-use cookie; the app tries a renewal twice before signing a device out. A lost device: change the password (it ends every session).

Details: docs\SECURITY.md.

14Keeping the guides up to date

Rule, since 26 October 2026: every change to the app or the server updates:

  • the User guide (docs\Tasks-user-guide.pdf), for anything a user sees;
  • this Admin guide (docs\Tasks-admin-guide.pdf), for anything about running the server;
  • docs\CHANGELOG.md, and the detailed docs\*.md concerned;
  • the app's Updates (src/lib/news.ts), for anything a user notices, in plain words: each device shows the new entries once after the update ("What's new" box), and ? → Updates keeps and searches them all. One feature, one place: when a feature is reworked, its lines are taken out of the older updates and the newest one describes it in full, as it is now; several changes on the same day make one update.
